Caveats that matter more than the headline number:
- **All 20 flagged links come from one entity**, because only one multi-wallet entity exists in the 7-day window. The sample effectively tests one clustering decision, made of 20 pairs, not 20 independent ones. It says little about precision on other operator styles.
- **A false positive was found and fixed before this review.** `0x6f37…` and `0xf359…` were merged because they shared two ancestors on one funding chain, and each was counted. That behaviour is fixed (one lineage counts once per pair). Without the fix, the sample would likely have included an FP.
